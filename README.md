# Where Do We Really Come From? — J.J. Aldren

A short-form history book examining Sumerian cuneiform tablet records and their account of human origins, including references to the Anunnaki and pre-biblical creation narratives.

The book addresses the gap between mainstream historical accounts of early civilisation and the specific claims encoded in the oldest written records on Earth. Drawing on the work of researchers including Graham Hancock, Mauro Biglino, and Zecharia Sitchin's translation of the Enuma Elish.

The book reached number one in its Amazon category on release and is available in Kindle and print formats.
