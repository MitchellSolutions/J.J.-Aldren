6,000 years ago, the Sumerians didn't write myths. They kept records. While modern history dismisses their accounts of 'beings from the stars' as metaphor, the raw data of their sudden technological explosion suggests a different story.

They described beings from another world who arrived on Earth, built the first cities and created humanity itself through genetic engineering. They named them, recorded their disagreements, and explained exactly why we were made.

This book tells that story, drawing on the clay tablets, the stone carvings, and the work of serious researchers who spent decades translating them. It asks the question that schools and churches were never designed to answer:

JJ Aldren investigates the mandatory question: What if they were telling the truth?

Based on the research of Zecharia Sitchin, Anton Parks, Michael Tellinger, Graham Hancock, and ancient records including the Kebra Nagast and the Book of Enoch.
About the Author

J.J. Aldren

J.J. Aldren spent years exploring ancient civilisations, spiritual traditions, and alternative perspectives on human origins. With a foundation in religion and a lifelong drive to question, research, and connect hidden patterns across history and myth, they bring a thoughtful voice to complex ideas. This book is for younger readers and adults alike, especially those with neurodiverse ways of thinking who are drawn to depth, pattern recognition, and perspectives beyond the conventional.
